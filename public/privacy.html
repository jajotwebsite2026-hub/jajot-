Privacy Policy - Social Hub

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Social Hub is a private tool operated by AG Technicals (Ajmer, India) that lets a business publish its own posts to its own social media accounts from one dashboard.

What we collect

  • Account details of the dashboard user: name, email, and a hashed password.
  • Posts you write and media you upload, so they can be published and shown in History.
  • Access tokens issued by social platforms (for example Pinterest, Threads, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, Google Business) after you approve the connection. We also store the name of the connected account or board.

How we use it

Only to publish and schedule the posts you create, on the accounts you connected. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is kept on our server in a private database. Tokens are used only to post on your behalf. Access to the dashboard requires login.

Your control

You can disconnect any account by removing it in the Accounts tab, and revoke access in the settings of the platform itself. To delete your data, contact us and we will remove it.
